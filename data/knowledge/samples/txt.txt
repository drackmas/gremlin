# library/ — knowledge library source

Drop files you want Gremlin to be able to answer questions from in here, then
click **Sync** in Settings → Knowledge (or run `python -m knowledge.sync`).
The index under `data/knowledge/` is disposable; this folder is the source of
truth.

Supported: `.txt` `.md` `.pdf` `.docx` `.doc` `.epub` `.mp3` `.wav` `.m4a`
`.mp4` `.webm`. Subfolders are fine and preserved in citations.

Conventions for plain text:
- Name it descriptively: `manners-of-women-1908-ch3.txt` → title + year.
