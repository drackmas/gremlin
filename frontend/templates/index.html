Speech-to-Text
Transcribe your voice with a local model — Whisper (faster-whisper) or Parakeet-TDT (onnx-asr). Runs on CPU; models live in models/stt/.
Hold: press the mic, speak, release to fill the input box. Continuous: tap the mic to listen; each sentence is transcribed and sent automatically after a pause.
Parakeet-TDT is fast and accurate (English / multilingual); Whisper models are slower.
Models live in models/stt/ — see its README.
Threshold: how loud you must be (0–1) to be recognized as speech. Silence: the pause that ends an utterance in Continuous mode. Starting to speak always interrupts any running reply, so you can barge in at any time.

Settings are saved to this machine and restored on every reload and restart.
